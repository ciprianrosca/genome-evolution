# Head of Engineering, Payments: Bluewater Pay (made up)

Bluewater Pay is a made-up payments company in Cluj. We process card and bank payments for 2,000 small online shops.

## The role
You will lead our payments engineering group: three teams, about 20 engineers, and their team leads. You report
to the CTO.

## What you will do
- Keep payments fast and reliable while the volume doubles.
- Grow the team leads and hire the next ten engineers.
- Move the ledger off a monolith without losing a single transaction.

## What we look for
- You have led several engineering teams, ideally in payments or another regulated field.
- You have run a large data migration in production.
- You know Kotlin or Java, Kafka and PostgreSQL well enough to review designs.
- Experience with PCI DSS audits is a plus.
