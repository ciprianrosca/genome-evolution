# Director of Engineering: Kestrel Freight (made up)

Kestrel Freight is a made-up logistics company whose software books and tracks lorry freight across Europe.

## The role
You will run engineering (four teams) and work with product on a two-year plan to replace our booking system.

## What we look for
- You have led several teams and their leads.
- You have shipped a replacement of a core system without stopping the business.
- You care about delivery speed: small releases, good pipelines, few incidents.
- One-page resumes, please.
