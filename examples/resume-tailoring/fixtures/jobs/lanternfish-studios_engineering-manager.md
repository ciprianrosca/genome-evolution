# Engineering Manager, Live Games Backend: Lanternfish Studios (made up)

Lanternfish Studios is a made-up mobile games studio with four live puzzle and word games.

## The role
You will manage the backend team (8 engineers) that runs our games' servers, player accounts and in-game store.

## What we look for
- Experience managing a backend team for a live game or another consumer app with millions of players.
- A track record of improving stability: crash rates, incidents, on-call load.
- You hire well and help engineers grow.
- Java or Kotlin on the server; Kubernetes on AWS.
