# VP of Engineering: Orbitgrid (made up)

Orbitgrid is a made-up energy-trading platform growing from 40 to 120 engineers.

## What we require
- 15+ years in software, including at least 5 as a director or VP.
- You have led an organisation of 80 or more engineers through several managers.
- Hands-on experience with Go and with Kubernetes; a CKA certification is required.
- You have taken part in a funding round and presented to a board.

## Nice to have
- Energy or commodities trading.
- Experience setting up an engineering office in a new country.
