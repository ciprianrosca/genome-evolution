# Engineering Manager, Billing: Tidewell Health (made up)

Tidewell Health is a made-up network of clinics that is rebuilding how patients and insurers are billed.

## The role
You will manage the billing team (6 engineers) and own invoicing, payment plans and reconciliation with insurers.

## What we look for
- You have built or run a billing or invoicing system.
- You have managed engineers and hired for your team.
- Java and PostgreSQL.

## How to apply
Send a resume and a short cover letter (under 250 words) saying why this role.
